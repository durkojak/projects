~Our Mission~

NTU Delivery Service aims to provide fast and reliable food delivery within the NTU campus. Our mission is to ensure that students and staff can enjoy their favorite meals without leaving their location.

~Our Team~

We are a dedicated team of NTU students committed to enhancing campus life through our delivery service. Our team works tirelessly to ensure timely deliveries and excellent customer service.
